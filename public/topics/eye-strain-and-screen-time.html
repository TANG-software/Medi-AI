Eye strain & screen timeUsually home care

What it is

Digital eye strain causes tired, dry, aching eyes, blurred vision and headaches after long screen sessions. It is uncomfortable but does not permanently damage the eyes.

What you can do

Follow the 20-20-20 rule: every 20 minutes look 20 feet away for 20 seconds. Blink often, keep screens slightly below eye level, reduce glare and use lubricating eye drops if dry. Sudden vision loss or eye pain is different — seek care.
