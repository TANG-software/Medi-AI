Interaction: statins + certain antibioticsSee a doctor

What it is

Clarithromycin/erythromycin-type antibiotics raise statin levels, risking serious muscle damage — unexplained muscle pain, weakness or dark urine.

What you can do

Always tell the doctor prescribing an antibiotic that you take a statin. New muscle pain after starting any new medicine deserves a check.
