Peptic ulcer & H. pyloriSee a doctor

What it is

Peptic ulcers are sores in the stomach/duodenal lining, often from H. pylori bacteria or regular painkiller use. Classic burning upper abdominal pain often improves with food and returns hours later.

What you can do

Get medical evaluation — H. pylori needs a tested course of medicines. Black/tarry stools, coffee-ground vomit or sudden severe abdominal pain means urgent hospital care.

Common signs people search for

ulcer · stomach · burning · peptic · gastritis · pylori · pain · hunger · black · stool
