Metangy health guide · Bones, joints & muscles

Sprains & strainsUsually home care

What it is

Stretched or torn ligaments/muscles causing pain and swelling around a joint.

What you can do

Follow RICE for 2-3 days: Rest, Ice 15 min hourly (wrapped in cloth), Compression bandage, Elevation. Doctor/X-ray if you cannot bear weight, the joint looks deformed, or it is not improving in a week.

Common signs people search for

sprain · strain · twisted · ankle · wrist · injury · swelling · bruise

Reference: NHS

Important: this is general health information, not a diagnosis. Metangy can explain a topic or a lab report, but a qualified professional must diagnose and treat. In an emergency call 112 (India).
