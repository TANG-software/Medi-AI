Near-drowningUsually home care

What it is

Water accidents kill by suffocation; every near-drowning needs medical review even if the person seems fine.

What you can do

Get the person out of water safely (reach or throw, do not jump in unless trained). If not breathing, call 112 and start CPR. Hospital check is a must for all — lung problems can develop hours later.

Common signs people search for

drowning · water · rescue · pool · river · fell · water

Reference: NHS
