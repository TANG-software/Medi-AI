Metangy health guide · Mental health

DepressionSee a doctor

What it is

More than 2 weeks of low mood, loss of interest, sleep and appetite changes, fatigue, worthlessness.

What you can do

Talk to someone you trust and see a doctor or counsellor — it is treatable. If there are thoughts of self-harm, seek help immediately: Tele-MANAS 14416 (India, 24x7).

Common signs people search for

depression · sad · hopeless · sleep · loss · interest · low · mood

Important: this is general health information, not a diagnosis. Metangy can explain a topic or a lab report, but a qualified professional must diagnose and treat. In an emergency call 112 (India).
