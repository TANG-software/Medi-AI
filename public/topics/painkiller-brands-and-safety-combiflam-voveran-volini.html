Painkiller brands & safety (Combiflam, Voveran, Volini)Usually home care

What it is

Combiflam = ibuprofen + paracetamol; Voveran = diclofenac. These NSAIDs reduce pain and swelling but can harm the stomach, kidneys and blood pressure with frequent use, and can be risky in dengue and kidney disease.

What you can do

Use the lowest dose for the shortest time, with food. Gels like Volini/Moov are safer for muscle pain than tablets. People with kidney disease, gastritis, heart issues or on blood thinners should ask a doctor first. Recurring pain needs a diagnosis, not repeated painkillers.
