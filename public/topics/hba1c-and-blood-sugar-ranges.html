HbA1c and blood sugar rangesSee a doctor

What it is

HbA1c shows average blood sugar over ~3 months: below 5.7% is normal, 5.7–6.4% is pre-diabetes, 6.5% or above on two tests suggests diabetes. Fasting sugar 70–100 mg/dL is normal; 100–125 is pre-diabetic; above 126 suggests diabetes. Post-meal (PP) values above 200 mg/dL are also suggestive.

What you can do

Diagnosis and treatment need a doctor — never start or stop diabetes medicines yourself. Pre-diabetes can often be reversed with diet, weight loss and 30 minutes of daily walking.
