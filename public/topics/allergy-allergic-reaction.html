Metangy health guide · Breathing, nose & lungs

Allergy / allergic reactionSee a doctor

What it is

Itchy rash, sneezing or swelling after exposure to a trigger.

What you can do

Antihistamine tablets help mild cases. EMERGENCY (call 112) if lips/tongue swell or breathing is affected — this is anaphylaxis.

Common signs people search for

allergy · itching · rash · hives · swelling · sneezing · peanut · dust

Important: this is general health information, not a diagnosis. Metangy can explain a topic or a lab report, but a qualified professional must diagnose and treat. In an emergency call 112 (India).
