Metangy health guide · Women’s health

Breastfeeding basicsUsually home care

What it is

Breast milk alone is ideal food for the first 6 months; feeding often builds supply.

What you can do

Feed on demand day and night (8-12 times), ensure a deep latch, no bottles/water till 6 months, then family foods alongside up to 2 years. Support and rest help supply more than any "galactagogue". Lactation counsellor if nipples crack or baby gains poorly.

Common signs people search for

breastfeeding · milk · supply · latch · colic · weaning

Reference: WHO
