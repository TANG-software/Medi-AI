Sore throat / tonsillitisSee a doctor

What it is

Most sore throats are viral and settle in 3–7 days. Tonsillitis with fever, swollen neck glands and white patches on the tonsils can be bacterial and may need antibiotics after examination.

What you can do

Warm salt-water gargles, warm fluids and honey soothe. See a doctor for trouble breathing or swallowing, drooling, high fever with white patches, or symptoms beyond a week. Antibiotics must be prescribed, never self-started.
