Privacy Policy

Last updated: 4 October 2026

Medi AI is a health information assistant. Because people ask health questions and upload medical reports here, we take privacy seriously. This page explains, in plain language, what we collect, why, who processes it, and what you can do about it.

Medi AI is not a medical service. It gives general health information only and does not replace a doctor. In an emergency, call 112 (India) or your local emergency number.

1. What we collect

  • Account details: your username, email address and a securely hashed password. We never store your password in plain text.
  • Your questions and chats: the messages you send and the answers you receive, so you can read them again later.
  • Reports you scan: the text extracted from a report photo and the explanation generated for it, saved to your account so you can revisit it.
  • Credits and plan information: how many credits you have, your plan and its renewal date.
  • Basic technical data: the kind of browser and device used, needed for the app to work and to keep it safe from abuse.
  • Local storage on your device: small values such as your chosen reply language and voice settings. These stay on your device.

2. How your questions are processed

To answer you, the text of your question (and, for a report scan, the text extracted from your report) is sent to third-party AI model providers. They process it only to generate the answer. Medi AI is built not to invent values it cannot read — if a report cannot be read, it will say so instead of guessing.

3. What happens to your report photos

Text is read from your report photo on your own device (in your browser). The photo itself is not uploaded to our servers by default; what is saved to your account is the extracted text and the explanation, so you can find the report again later.

4. Payments

Paid plans are processed by Razorpay. Your card, UPI or bank details are handled by Razorpay and are never stored by Medi AI. We receive only the confirmation that a payment succeeded, along with basic order details.

5. Advertising

Free accounts may see advertising from third-party networks (such as Adsterra, and Google AdSense where enabled). These networks may use cookies or similar technologies to show and measure ads. They do not receive your chats or your reports from us. You can limit personalised advertising in your browser or device settings, or use a paid plan, which shows no ads.

6. Who we share data with

We do not sell your personal data. We share it only with the service providers that make Medi AI work:

  • AI model providers — to generate your answers.
  • Razorpay — to process payments.
  • Advertising networks — for ads shown to free accounts.
  • Hosting and database providers — to store your account, chats and reports securely.

We may also disclose information if the law requires it.

7. Health information and your responsibility

Your questions may contain health information about you or someone else. Please avoid including names, phone numbers, ID numbers or other identifying details of other people unless you have their permission.

8. Children and young users

Medi AI is intended for users aged 13 and above. If you are under 18, please use Medi AI only with the permission and supervision of a parent or guardian. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13; if you believe a child under 13 has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

9. How long we keep data

Chats and reports stay in your account until you delete them or ask us to delete your account. Deleting a chat or report removes it from your account. Payment records may be kept for the period required by law.

10. Your choices and rights

  • Delete individual chats and saved reports inside the app.
  • Change your reply language and voice settings at any time.
  • Ask us to correct or delete your account and its data by emailing us.
  • Use a paid plan if you prefer not to see ads.

11. Security

Passwords are stored as one-way hashes, and access to the database is restricted. No online service can promise perfect security, so please use a strong, unique password and avoid sharing sensitive identifying details in your questions.

12. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes in an important way, we will update the date at the top of this page. Continued use of Medi AI after a change means you accept the updated policy.

13. Contact

Questions about privacy, or want your data deleted? Email tangydust@zohomail.in.
