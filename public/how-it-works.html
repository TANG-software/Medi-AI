How Metangy works

Medi AI · models: Metangy · Last updated: 10 October 2026

Metangy is a web assistant for everyday health questions, built to be clear, careful and multilingual. Here is what happens behind the scenes when you use it.

Metangy gives general health information — it is not a medical diagnosis and does not replace a doctor. In an emergency, call 112 (India) or your local emergency number.

Asking a question

You type a question the way you would ask a friend — symptoms, medicines, a report you do not understand. You do not choose a language: Metangy detects what you wrote (Hindi, English, Tamil, Telugu, Bengali, Marathi, Malayalam, Kannada, Gujarati, Punjabi, Hinglish and many more) and answers in the same language. Questions cost 1 credit; a report scan costs 2.

10 AI models, one answer

Metangy does not depend on a single AI model — it runs on 10 AI models. They work together: for each question they produce answers in parallel, and the first good, complete answer is used while the others act as backup. If one model is slow or unavailable, the others still answer — so you are not left waiting. Higher plans use more models working together.

Report Lens — reading a lab report

1. The photo stays on your device. When you photograph a lab report, the text is extracted in your own browser — the image itself is not uploaded to our servers.

2. The image is prepared first. Small phone photos are enlarged, colours are removed and contrast is boosted, because OCR reads that far better than a dim colour photo.

3. Sideways photos are handled. If a report is photographed rotated, the first reading comes out as gibberish — so Metangy checks whether the text looks like real report text, and if not it automatically rotates the image 90°, 180° and 270° and reads again, keeping the best result.

4. You check the text. The extracted text is shown to you before analysis, and you can correct any mistakes.

5. You get a structured explanation: what the report is, a table of key results with normal ranges, what they may mean in plain words, common causes, and how to improve — plus a clear reminder that it is not a diagnosis.

6. It never guesses. Metangy is instructed not to invent a value it cannot read; if the text is genuinely unreadable, it says so and suggests how to get a clearer copy.

Listening instead of reading

Every answer has a read-aloud button. Metangy uses a natural AI voice where available and falls back to your phone's own voice if the premium voice cannot be reached — so reading aloud works even on a weak connection.

Credits and plans

Free accounts receive credits on a weekly cycle. Paid plans add daily credits, more AI engines and no ads. Payments are handled by Razorpay (UPI, cards, netbanking, wallets), and visitors from outside India also see an approximate price in their own currency.

Trying it without an account

On the login page you can ask up to 3 questions a day without signing up. Guest questions are answered but not saved anywhere. Creating a free account gives you credits every week and saves your chats and reports so you can return to them.

Safety built in

  • Emergency phrases are detected and answered with an immediate "seek help now" warning.
  • Answers about medicines never include dosages to self-prescribe.
  • Every answer carries the reminder that Metangy is not a diagnosis.
  • Chats and reports are stored in your account, and you can delete them.

Everything included

  • Medical chat in 50+ languages with automatic language detection.
  • Report Lens — a photo of a lab report becomes a plain-language explanation, read on your own device.
  • 10 AI models with automatic failover; higher plans run more of them per answer.
  • 160+ curated health topics and lab-report guides, with sources such as the NHS and WHO.
  • Voice input and read-aloud answers.
  • Emergency detection, saved reports and chats, a personal dashboard, and report-a-problem.
  • Guest mode — 3 free credits without an account.
  • Invite 2 friends and get Plus free.
  • Installable web app (PWA) and a WhatsApp bot.

What it is not

Metangy is not a hospital, a doctor or an emergency service. It helps you understand health information and ask better questions. Diagnosis and treatment must come from a qualified professional.
