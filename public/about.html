About Metangy

Medi AI's flagship model · Last updated: 10 October 2026

Metangy is a free health assistant for anyone who has ever stared at a medical report, a prescription or a symptom and wished someone would just explain it in plain words — in the language they actually think in.

Powered by 10 AI models. Ten separate AI models (engines) work together behind every answer — with automatic failover, so a reply still comes even if one is slow or unavailable.

Built by tangydust (Nirvan), a 14-year-old solo developer, over six months. Metangy went live in September 2026.

Why it exists

Most health information online is written for people who already understand medical English. In India, that leaves out an enormous number of people: those who read Hindi, Tamil, Telugu, Bengali or any other language more comfortably than English, and anyone who simply wants a straight answer without twenty open tabs.

Metangy was built to close that gap: ask in your own language, get a clear structured answer, and if you have a lab report, have it explained line by line — including the parts that matter and the parts that do not.

What makes it different

  • It answers in your language, automatically detecting what you wrote.
  • Report Lens reads a photo of a lab report on your device and explains the values against their normal ranges.
  • 10 AI models work together so an answer still comes even if one model is slow.
  • A curated knowledge base of over 160 health topics and lab-report guides, with sources such as the NHS, supports the answers.
  • It is careful on purpose: it does not invent unreadable values, does not give dosages to self-prescribe, and warns you immediately when a question sounds like an emergency.

Everything it does

  • Medical chat — ask any health question in 50+ languages; Metangy detects your language and replies in it.
  • Report Lens — photograph a lab report; the text is read on your device (with automatic rotation) and explained value by value against normal ranges.
  • 10 AI models — ten AI models work together with automatic failover, so an answer still comes even if one is slow.
  • Curated knowledge base — 160+ health topics and lab-report guides, with sources such as the NHS and WHO.
  • Voice input and read-aloud — speak your question, and have answers read back in a natural AI voice.
  • Emergency detection — warns you immediately when a question sounds like an emergency (call 112 in India).
  • Saved reports and chat history, plus a personal dashboard.
  • Guest mode — try the full app with 3 free credits, no account needed.
  • Invite friends — invite 2 friends who create an account and get Plus free.
  • Installable as a web app (PWA), with a WhatsApp bot.

Languages: 50+ — all 22 official Indian languages (Hindi, Tamil, Telugu, Bengali, Marathi, Malayalam, Kannada, Gujarati, Punjabi, Odia, Assamese, Urdu and more), Hinglish, and major world languages.

Pricing: a free plan with weekly credits, and paid plans that add daily credits, more AI models per answer and ad-free use. Payments are handled by Razorpay (UPI, cards, netbanking, wallets).

Who builds it

Medi AI is a multilingual health AI. Like other AI systems that ship several models, Medi AI's flagship model is Metangy — the one you are using here.

Metangy answers in 50+ languages, reads photos of lab reports, and draws on a curated medical knowledge base. It is built by tangydust (Nirvan), a 14-year-old solo developer, over six months — it went live in September 2026. It is not owned by a hospital, a clinic or a large corporation, which is exactly why every report of a problem is read carefully and acted on.

What it is not

Metangy is not a doctor and not a diagnosis. It is general health information, a second pair of eyes on a confusing document, and a way to prepare better questions for a real consultation. In an emergency, always call 112 (India) or your local emergency number.
