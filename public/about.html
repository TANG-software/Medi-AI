About Medi AI

Last updated: 4 October 2026

Medi AI is a free health assistant for anyone who has ever stared at a medical report, a prescription or a symptom and wished someone would just explain it in plain words — in the language they actually think in.

Why it exists

Most health information online is written for people who already understand medical English. In India, that leaves out an enormous number of people: those who read Hindi, Tamil, Telugu, Bengali or any other language more comfortably than English, and anyone who simply wants a straight answer without twenty open tabs.

Medi AI was built to close that gap: ask in your own language, get a clear structured answer, and if you have a lab report, have it explained line by line — including the parts that matter and the parts that do not.

What makes it different

  • It answers in your language, automatically detecting what you wrote.
  • Report Lens reads a photo of a lab report on your device and explains the values against their normal ranges.
  • Several AI engines work together so an answer still comes even if one engine is slow.
  • A curated knowledge base of over 160 health topics and lab-report guides, with sources such as the NHS, supports the answers.
  • It is careful on purpose: it does not invent unreadable values, does not give dosages to self-prescribe, and warns you immediately when a question sounds like an emergency.

Who builds it

Medi AI is an independent project built and maintained in India. It is not owned by a hospital, a clinic or a large company — it is a small, focused effort that grows from user feedback. That is also why every report of a problem is read carefully and acted on.

What it is not

Medi AI is not a doctor and not a diagnosis. It is general health information, a second pair of eyes on a confusing document, and a way to prepare better questions for a real consultation. In an emergency, always call 112 (India) or your local emergency number.
