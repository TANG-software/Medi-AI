Frequently asked questions

Last updated: 4 October 2026

Medi AI gives general health information — it is not a medical diagnosis and does not replace a doctor. In an emergency, call 112 (India) or your local emergency number immediately.

What is Medi AI?

Medi AI is a free web assistant for everyday health questions. You can ask about symptoms, medicines or lab reports in your own language and get a clear, structured answer. It also has Report Lens, which reads a photo of a medical report and explains the results in plain language.

Is Medi AI free?

Yes — you can start for free. Every free account gets a set number of credits each week. A normal question costs 1 credit and a report scan costs 2 credits. If you need more, paid plans add daily credits and more AI engines working together.

Which languages does it support?

Over 50 languages, including Hindi, English, Tamil, Telugu, Bengali, Marathi, Malayalam, Kannada, Gujarati and Punjabi. You do not have to choose a language — type in whatever language you are comfortable with (including Hinglish) and Medi AI replies in the same language.

How does Report Lens work?

You take a photo of a lab report (or pick one from your gallery). The text is read on your own device, then the extracted text is sent for explanation. You can correct any mis-read text before analysing. Medi AI then explains what the report is, lists the key values with their normal ranges, and explains what they may mean — in simple words. Sideways or rotated photos are handled automatically.

Can I trust the answers?

Medi AI is built to be careful: it is told never to invent values it cannot read, and to say clearly when something is not readable. Still, AI can make mistakes. Always confirm important findings with a qualified doctor, and never delay medical care because of something you read here.

Can it replace a doctor?

No. Medi AI helps you understand health information and prepare better questions for your doctor. Diagnosis and treatment must come from a qualified professional.

What happens in an emergency?

Do not use Medi AI in an emergency. Call 112 (India) or your local emergency number right away. If a question looks like an emergency, Medi AI will show a warning telling you to seek immediate help.

Do I need an account?

Yes — a free account is needed to use the chat and to save your reports so you can come back to them. Signing up only needs a username, an email and a password.

Is my health information private?

Your chats and reports are stored in your account so you can revisit them, and your question text is sent to AI providers only to generate your answer. We do not sell your data. Full details are in our Privacy Policy.

Can I delete my chats and reports?

Yes. You can delete individual conversations and saved reports from inside the app. To delete your whole account and its data, contact us at the email below.

How do I pay for a plan?

Payments are handled by Razorpay, so you can pay with UPI, cards, netbanking or wallets. Prices are shown in Indian Rupees, with an approximate price in your local currency for visitors from other countries.

Something is not working. How do I report it?

Use Report a problem inside the app — it reaches us directly with the details. You can also email us.

Who builds Medi AI?

Medi AI is an independent project built and maintained in India. It is not backed by a hospital or a company, which is why your feedback matters so much.
