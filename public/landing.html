Metangy gives general health information, not a medical diagnosis. In an emergency call 112 (India).

What you can do

  • Ask anything — symptoms, medicines, a report you don't understand. It replies in the language you write in.
  • Report Lens — photograph a lab report; the text is read on your device and each value is explained against its normal range.
  • Listen — answers can be read aloud, and you can speak your question instead of typing.
  • Stay safe — if something sounds like an emergency, Metangy stops and tells you to call 112.
