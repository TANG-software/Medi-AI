Delivery & Shipping Policy

Last updated: 5 October 2026

Metangy is a digital service. Nothing is shipped physically — there is no courier, no delivery address and no shipping charge. What you buy is access inside the app, and it is added to your account the moment your payment succeeds.

1. What is delivered

  • Credits — used for questions (1 credit) and report scans (2 credits).
  • Plan access — the larger credit allowance, extra AI engines and ad-free use for the period you paid for (for example 7 or 28 days).

2. How and when it is delivered

  • Delivery method: online, inside your Metangy account. There is no physical shipment.
  • Delivery time: immediately after a successful payment — usually within seconds. Your credits and plan appear in the app without any waiting period.
  • Confirmation: the app shows your updated credits and plan straight away. If you have an email on your account, your payment receipt is sent by our payment partner, Razorpay.
  • Access anywhere: the same account works on any modern browser, on phone or desktop.

3. Shipping charges and address

  • No shipping charges. The price you see is the full amount you pay.
  • No delivery address is needed — we never ask for one, because nothing is shipped.
  • There are no courier partners, tracking numbers or delivery windows.

4. If delivery fails

In the rare case that your payment succeeds but the credits do not appear:

  • Wait up to 30 minutes and refresh the app — most delays are just a slow connection.
  • If they still have not appeared, email tangydust@zohomail.in with your username and the payment or order ID.
  • We fix it or refund it in full. We reply within 3 business days, and approved refunds go back to your original payment method within 5–10 business days.

5. Related policies

See also our Refund & Cancellation Policy, Terms of Use and Privacy Policy.

6. Contact

Delivery or billing question? Email tangydust@zohomail.in.
