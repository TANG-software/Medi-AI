Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated: 5 October 2026

This policy explains how Metangy's paid plans work, how they are delivered, and when a refund can be requested. It applies to every purchase made through Metangy.

Metangy is a digital service. Nothing is shipped physically — credits and plan access are added to your account instantly after a successful payment. In an emergency, do not use Metangy: call 112 (India) or your local emergency number.

1. What you are buying

  • Credits: one question costs 1 credit; a report scan (Report Lens) costs 2 credits. Free accounts get a set number of credits each week at no cost.
  • Paid plans: add a larger number of credits that refresh on a daily cycle for a fixed period (for example 7 or 28 days), plus more AI engines and no advertising.
  • Plan prices and the credits included are always shown on the Pricing page before you pay. Prices are charged in Indian Rupees (₹).

2. Delivery policy

  • Access is delivered immediately after a successful payment — your credits and plan appear in your account without any shipping or waiting period.
  • There are no physical goods, no courier charges and no shipping address required.
  • If a payment succeeds but your credits do not appear within 30 minutes, email us with your payment/order ID and we will fix it or refund you.

3. Cancellation

  • Paid plans are prepaid for a fixed period. There is no automatic renewal unless it is clearly shown at checkout.
  • You can stop using Metangy at any time. To cancel a plan that renews, email us before the next renewal date and we will stop it.
  • Cancelling does not remove access you have already paid for — your plan stays active until the end of the period you paid for.

4. When we refund

We refund in full, without argument, when the fault is ours:

  • You were charged twice for the same purchase.
  • The payment succeeded but the credits were never added to your account and we could not fix it.
  • A technical fault on our side made the plan unusable for the period you paid for.
  • A duplicate or accidental purchase that you report to us within 7 days, where the credits from that purchase have not been used.

5. When we cannot refund

  • Credits that have already been used (questions asked, reports scanned).
  • Change of mind after the credits from that purchase have been partly or fully used.
  • Problems caused by something outside the service — for example a device or internet problem, or disagreement with a general health answer. Metangy gives general information, not a diagnosis.

6. How to request a refund

  • Email tangydust@zohomail.in with your username, the payment or order ID, the date and the amount.
  • We reply within 3 business days.
  • Approved refunds are returned to the same payment method you used, usually within 5–10 business days, depending on your bank or card issuer.
  • Please contact us before raising a chargeback with your bank — almost every issue is solved faster by email, and a chargeback can take weeks.

7. Your rights

Nothing in this policy limits any right you have under applicable consumer law in India. Where the law gives you a stronger right than this policy, the law applies.

8. Contact

Refund or billing question? Email tangydust@zohomail.in. See also our Terms of Use and Privacy Policy.
