Health topics A–Z

Last updated: 4 October 2026

These are the topics Medi AI can explain in plain language. Each entry is a short summary of a general health subject — open Medi AI and ask about any of them in your own language for a fuller explanation.

This is general educational information, not a medical diagnosis. It is not a substitute for advice from a qualified doctor. If something is severe or getting worse, or you are unsure, see a doctor. In an emergency call 112 (India).
A

Acidity / GERDSelf-care

Stomach acid moving up causes burning behind the breastbone, worse lying down or after spicy/fatty meals.

Acne (pimples)Self-care

Clogged oil glands — driven by hormones; not caused by eating specific foods (chocolate myth) or dirt.

Acute bronchitisSelf-care

A chesty cough following a cold, often lasting 2-3 weeks; usually viral so antibiotics rarely help.

ADHD basicsSee a doctor

ADHD is a neurodevelopmental difference: inattention, hyperactivity, impulsivity affecting school and home.

Adult vaccinationSelf-care

Adults need vaccines too: tetanus every 10 years, yearly flu (elderly/asthma/diabetes), hepatitis B, pneumococcal after 60, HPV for young adults.

Air pollution & healthSee a doctor

Delhi-NCR winters and city AQI above 200 harm everyone — worse for asthma, heart, pregnancy and children.

Alcohol harm & cutting downSee a doctor

Regular heavy drinking damages liver, brain, heart and family life; safe level for some conditions is zero.

Allergy / allergic reactionSee a doctor

Itchy rash, sneezing or swelling after exposure to a trigger.

Anaemia in pregnancySee a doctor

Low haemoglobin in pregnancy risks mother and baby — common in India and fully preventable.

Anemia (low hemoglobin)See a doctor

Low hemoglobin reduces oxygen carrying capacity — tiredness, paleness, breathlessness on effort.

Antibiotic misuseSelf-care

Antibiotics do NOT work on colds, flu, most coughs, loose motions or fevers caused by viruses.

Anxiety and panicSelf-care

Panic attacks mimic heart trouble: racing heart, breathlessness, tingling — but are not dangerous and peak within 10 minutes.

Appendicitis warning signsUrgent — get help

Appendicitis starts as tummy-button pain moving to the lower right side with vomiting and low fever.

AsthmaSee a doctor

Asthma narrows airways causing wheeze and cough, often triggered by dust, cold or pollen.

Autism red flagsSee a doctor

Early signs by 12-18 months: no pointing/name response, poor eye contact, no words by 16 months, repetitive play.

B

Back painSelf-care

Most back pain is muscular, improves in 1-2 weeks with gentle movement.

BedwettingSelf-care

Night wetting is common up to age 7 and usually resolves by itself.

Bee / wasp stingSee a doctor

Most stings cause local pain and swelling; some cause severe allergic reaction.

Blood donationSelf-care

Healthy 18-65 year olds (45kg+) can donate every 3 months — one unit can save three lives.

Breast lump awarenessSee a doctor

Most breast lumps are benign, but any NEW lump needs a doctor visit — early breast cancer is highly curable.

Breastfeeding basicsSelf-care

Breast milk alone is ideal food for the first 6 months; feeding often builds supply.

Breathlessness / difficulty breathingUrgent — get help

Sudden or severe difficulty breathing is an emergency sign whatever the cause.

Broken bone (fracture)Urgent — get help

Suspect a break with a fall or crush injury: pain, swelling, deformity or inability to move/bear weight.

Burns (first aid)See a doctor

Cool the burn under running tap water for 20 minutes.

C

CataractSee a doctor

A cataract is clouding of the eye lens, mostly with age: gradual blurring, glare from lights, faded colors and frequent spectacle changes.

Celiac disease / glutenSee a doctor

Celiac disease is an immune reaction to gluten (wheat, barley, rye) damaging the small intestine: chronic diarrhoea, bloating, anemia and poor growth in children.

Cervical cancer & screeningSee a doctor

Cervical cancer is nearly 100% preventable by HPV vaccination (best at 9-14 years) and regular Pap smears.

Chest pain (possible heart attack)Urgent — get help

Chest pain with pressure, sweating, radiating to arm/jaw/back may be a heart attack — treat every minute as critical.

ChickenpoxSee a doctor

Itchy fluid-filled blisters appearing in crops with mild fever.

ChikungunyaSee a doctor

Mosquito-borne viral fever with severe joint pain that can linger for weeks.

Child fever (parents guide)See a doctor

In babies under 3 months ANY fever is an emergency.

Child immunisation (India)Self-care

India’s UIP gives free vaccines: BCG, Hep-B, OPV/IPV, pentavalent, rotavirus, PCV, MR, DPT boosters.

Child nutrition & picky eatingSelf-care

Toddlers famously eat unpredictably; growth (weight/height on chart) matters more than one meal.

Choking (first aid)Urgent — get help

Choking blocks the airway — acting within minutes saves lives.

CholeraUrgent — get help

Cholera causes sudden profuse watery diarrhoea that can kill through dehydration within hours.

Chronic kidney disease (CKD)See a doctor

CKD is gradual, permanent kidney weakening — usually silent for years.

Cold, cough & allergy brands (cetirizine, montelukast, cough syrups)Self-care

Cetirizine (Cetzine etc.) treats sneezing, runny nose and allergy — once a day, may cause mild sleepiness.

Common coldSelf-care

A viral infection of nose and throat lasting about a week.

Complete blood count (CBC) explainedSee a doctor

A CBC measures hemoglobin (oxygen-carrying capacity), WBC (infection-fighting cells), RBC, hematocrit and platelets (clotting cells).

Conjunctivitis (eye flu)See a doctor

Red, watery, sticky eyes spreading easily by touch.

ConstipationSelf-care

Fewer than 3 stools a week, or hard stools needing straining.

Contraception overviewSee a doctor

Many safe options exist: condoms, pills, copper-T/IUD, implants and permanent surgery.

COPD (smoker’s lung)See a doctor

Long-term lung damage, almost always from smoking/biomass smoke: progressive breathlessness and daily cough.

COVID-19See a doctor

COVID-19 is a respiratory viral infection; symptoms vary from mild cold to severe pneumonia.

D

DehydrationSelf-care

Dark little urine, dry mouth, dizziness — common with diarrhoea, vomiting or heat.

Dementia (memory loss)See a doctor

Progressive memory loss affecting daily life — beyond normal ageing forgetfulness.

Dengue feverUrgent — get help

Dengue is spread by Aedes mosquito bites; causes high fever, severe body and joint pain, sometimes rash after fever settles.

Dental / tooth painSee a doctor

Most toothache is from cavities or gum infection and needs a dentist.

DepressionSee a doctor

More than 2 weeks of low mood, loss of interest, sleep and appetite changes, fatigue, worthlessness.

Diabetes — daily self-careSee a doctor

Living with diabetes: control sugar to prevent eye, kidney, nerve and heart damage.

Diabetic retinopathy (eye check)See a doctor

High sugar damages the retina's tiny blood vessels — diabetic retinopathy is a top cause of blindness in working-age Indians.

Diarrhoea in childrenSee a doctor

Loose stools in children are mostly viral; the real danger is dehydration.

E

Ear pain / ear infectionSee a doctor

Pain, blockage or discharge; common after colds (especially in children).

Electric shockUrgent — get help

Electric shock can stop the heart or cause deep internal burns even when the skin looks fine.

Eye strain & screen timeSelf-care

Digital eye strain causes tired, dry, aching eyes, blurred vision and headaches after long screen sessions.

F

Fatty liverSee a doctor

Fat build-up in the liver, usually from weight, sugar/alcohol intake; often found on routine tests.

Fever (high temperature)Self-care

Fever means body temperature above 38 C (100.4 F), usually fighting an infection.

Food allergy vs food intoleranceSee a doctor

Food allergy is an immune reaction that can be severe (hives, lip/tongue swelling, vomiting, breathing trouble within minutes); intolerance (like lactose) is uncomfortable but not life-threatening — g…

Food poisoningSelf-care

Vomiting and diarrhoea within hours of suspect food; usually settles in 1-3 days.

Foreign body in the eyeSee a doctor

Dust or grit in the eye causes watering, redness and a gritty feeling.

Fungal skin infection (ringworm)Self-care

Itchy ring-shaped patches or pale scaly patches — spread by sweat, sharing towels, and steroid creams misuse.

G

GallstonesSee a doctor

Stones in the gallbladder cause attacks of severe right-upper-belly pain after fatty meals, sometimes with vomiting.

German measles (rubella)See a doctor

A mild viral rash with swollen neck glands — serious only if caught in early pregnancy.

Glaucoma (eye pressure)See a doctor

Glaucoma damages the optic nerve, usually due to high eye pressure.

GoutSee a doctor

Sudden severe painful red big toe (or other joints) from uric acid crystals — linked to beer, red meat, organ meat, dehydration.

Grief & lossSelf-care

Grief after losing someone is natural and takes months, with waves of pain, guilt or numbness.

H

Hair lossSee a doctor

Losing 50-100 hairs daily is normal. True loss shows as thinning patches or receding hairline.

Hand, foot and mouth diseaseSelf-care

A common childhood viral infection: small painful mouth ulcers and blisters on hands and feet.

HbA1c and blood sugar rangesSee a doctor

HbA1c shows average blood sugar over ~3 months: below 5.7% is normal, 5.7–6.4% is pre-diabetes, 6.5% or above on two tests suggests diabetes.

Head injury / concussionUrgent — get help

Any hit to the head needs watching; concussion may occur without losing consciousness.

Head liceSelf-care

Tiny insects on the scalp causing itching; spreads head-to-head at school.

Headache / migraineSelf-care

Most headaches are tension or migraine. Migraines are often one-sided with nausea and light sensitivity.

Healthy diet basicsSelf-care

An Indian thali done right: variety, more plants, adequate protein, less oil/salt/sugar.

Heart-healthy lifestyleSelf-care

Most heart attacks are preventable with habits: BP/sugar control, no tobacco, activity, diet.

Heat rash (prickly heat)Self-care

Blocked sweat ducts in hot humid weather — itchy tiny red bumps.

Heat stroke / heat exhaustionUrgent — get help

In extreme heat, confusion, hot dry skin or fainting means heat stroke — an emergency.

Hepatitis ASee a doctor

Liver infection spread by contaminated food/water causing jaundice, tiredness and stomach upset.

Hepatitis BSee a doctor

A liver virus spread by blood and body fluids; can become long-term and cause liver damage.

High blood pressure (hypertension)See a doctor

Usually silent — often found on routine checks.

High cholesterolSee a doctor

No symptoms — detected by blood test (lipid profile).

HIV/AIDS basicsSee a doctor

HIV spreads through unprotected sex, shared needles or from mother to baby — never by touch, sharing food or mosquitoes.

Home first aid kitSelf-care

Every home should have: bandages, gauze, antiseptic, ORS packets, paracetamol, digital thermometer, scissors, tweezers.

Home remedies: cold, cough & sore throatSelf-care

Traditional, generally safe supports: warm turmeric milk (haldi doodh), ginger-tulsi tea with honey (honey only above 1 year of age), salt-water gargles and plain steam inhalation.

Home remedies: digestion & aciditySelf-care

Commonly used and generally safe: jeera (cumin) water, ajwain (carom) with warm water, saunf (fennel) after meals, mint tea and eating slower.

Home remedies: hydration & summerSelf-care

For dehydration and heat: ORS packets are the gold standard; homemade alternatives include nimbu pani (lemon + sugar + a pinch of salt) and tender coconut water.

How to read a lab report safelySelf-care

Lab reports compare you against a reference range covering most healthy people — being slightly outside a range is common and often harmless.

I

Infertility basicsSee a doctor

About 1 in 6 couples face difficulty conceiving; causes can be from either partner equally.

Influenza (flu)See a doctor

Influenza is a viral illness with sudden fever, severe body aches and exhaustion; usually improves in 5-7 days.

Insomnia (sleep problems)Self-care

Difficulty falling or staying asleep for 3+ nights a week.

Interaction: antibiotics + antacids/iron/calciumSelf-care

Ciprofloxacin-type antibiotics bind to iron, calcium and antacids, losing effect.

Interaction: blood thinners + painkillersSee a doctor

Blood thinners (warfarin, clopidogrel, apixaban) plus NSAID painkillers (ibuprofen, diclofenac) sharply raise bleeding risk — stomach bleeds can occur with little warning.

Interaction: metformin + alcohol / scansSee a doctor

Metformin with heavy alcohol risks lactic acidosis (rare but dangerous).

Interaction: statins + certain antibioticsSee a doctor

Clarithromycin/erythromycin-type antibiotics raise statin levels, risking serious muscle damage — unexplained muscle pain, weakness or dark urine.

Intestinal wormsSelf-care

Common in children — tummy pain, itching around the anus, sometimes worms in stool.

Irregular periodsSee a doctor

Cycles outside 21-35 days, missed periods or unpredictable bleeding.

Irritable bowel syndrome (IBS)Self-care

A common gut disorder: cramping, bloating and alternating loose/hard stools, often stress-linked.

J

Jaundice / hepatitisSee a doctor

Yellowing of eyes/skin with dark urine means liver inflammation, often viral hepatitis.

K

Kidney function tests (creatinine, eGFR)See a doctor

Creatinine and urea are waste products cleared by the kidneys; eGFR estimates filtering capacity (90+ is normal for most adults).

Kidney stonesSee a doctor

Severe waves of pain from the side to the groin, sometimes blood in urine.

Knee joint pain / arthritisSelf-care

Joint pain with stiffness, worse in the morning or after activity.

L

Lactose intoleranceSelf-care

Lactose intolerance — very common in Indians — means the gut lacks the enzyme for milk sugar, causing bloating, cramps and diarrhoea within hours of milk.

LeptospirosisSee a doctor

Bacterial infection from water contaminated by animal urine — common after floods, causing fever with severe muscle pain.

Lipid profile (cholesterol test)See a doctor

The lipid profile measures LDL ("bad" cholesterol — lower is better), HDL ("good" — higher is better) and triglycerides.

Liver function tests (LFT)See a doctor

LFTs measure enzymes (ALT/SGPT, AST/SGOT), bilirubin and proteins that reflect liver health.

Living with epilepsySee a doctor

With regular medicines, 70% of people with epilepsy become seizure-free.

Low blood sugar (hypoglycemia)Urgent — get help

Sugar dropping too low (missed meals, extra medicine) is dangerous fast: sweating, shaking, confusion.

M

MalariaUrgent — get help

Malaria causes cyclical fever with shaking chills and heavy sweating, spread by Anopheles mosquitoes.

MeaslesSee a doctor

Highly contagious viral illness with fever, cough, red eyes and a rash starting behind the ears.

Medicine safetySelf-care

Expired, shared, or wrongly stored medicines cause poisoning and resistance.

MenopauseSelf-care

Natural change of hormones around 45-55: periods stop, hot flushes, sleep and mood changes.

Menstrual crampsSelf-care

Painful periods are common; usually start before the period and ease after day 1.

Mosquito-bite preventionSelf-care

Dengue/malaria/chikungunya all breed in clean/dirty standing water around homes.

Motion sicknessSelf-care

Motion sickness happens when the inner ear and eyes send conflicting movement signals — nausea, cold sweat and dizziness during car, bus or boat travel.

Mouth ulcersSee a doctor

Painful small sores in the mouth, usually stress/deficiency-linked, healing in 1-2 weeks.

MumpsSee a doctor

Viral infection causing painful swollen cheeks and jaw, mostly in children.

N

Near-drowningUrgent — get help

Water accidents kill by suffocation; every near-drowning needs medical review even if the person seems fine.

Neck & shoulder painSelf-care

Most neck pain is muscular — from phones, pillows, desks.

Newborn care basicsSee a doctor

First 28 days: exclusive breastfeeding, warmth, cord care, hygiene and vaccination at birth.

Nosebleed (epistaxis)See a doctor

Most nosebleeds are from a burst vessel in the front of the nose and stop with simple pressure.

O

Obesity & healthy weightSelf-care

Excess weight raises diabetes, BP, joint and cancer risk; even 5% loss helps measurably.

Osteoporosis (weak bones)See a doctor

Bones silently thinning with age (especially after menopause) until a small fall breaks a hip or wrist.

P

Painkiller (NSAID) safetySee a doctor

Paracetamol is safest at correct doses; NSAIDs (ibuprofen, diclofenac) can damage stomach, kidneys and BP with overuse.

Painkiller brands & safety (Combiflam, Voveran, Volini)Self-care

Combiflam = ibuprofen + paracetamol; Voveran = diclofenac.

Paracetamol brands in India (Crocin, Dolo, Calpol, P-500)Self-care

Crocin, Dolo-650, Calpol and P-500 all contain the same medicine — paracetamol — just from different companies.

Parkinson’s diseaseSee a doctor

A brain condition causing tremor at rest, slowness and stiffness — treatable for many years.

PCOS / PCODSee a doctor

A common hormone condition: irregular periods, weight gain, acne and excess facial hair; long-term diabetes risk.

Peptic ulcer & H. pyloriSee a doctor

Peptic ulcers are sores in the stomach/duodenal lining, often from H.

Piles (haemorrhoids)See a doctor

Swollen veins at the anus causing painless bright-red bleeding, itching or a lump.

PneumoniaUrgent — get help

Lung infection with fever, cough and fast or painful breathing — dangerous in young children and the elderly.

Poisoning / overdoseUrgent — get help

Swallowed poison needs immediate hospital care.

Pregnancy basicsSee a doctor

Pregnancy needs early antenatal registration, iron-folic acid tablets and 4 ultrasound visits as advised.

PsoriasisSee a doctor

An immune condition causing well-defined scaly patches — not contagious.

Psychosis awarenessSee a doctor

Sudden loss of touch with reality (hearing voices, strange beliefs, social withdrawal) is treatable — not "ghosts" or character failure.

R

Rabies (animal bite)Urgent — get help

Any dog, cat, monkey or bat bite/scratch can transmit rabies, which is 100% fatal once symptoms start — but 100% preventable with timely vaccination.

S

Safe drinking water & food hygieneSelf-care

Most stomach infections enter through unsafe water/food — prevention is cheap and proven.

ScabiesSee a doctor

Tiny mites burrowing in skin — intense night itching between fingers, wrists, waist; spreads by contact to whole families.

Seizure / fitsUrgent — get help

Most seizures stop on their own in 1-2 minutes.

Self-harm awarenessUrgent — get help

Self-harm is a distress signal, not attention-seeking — it needs kindness and professional support, never scolding.

Severe bleeding / heavy bleedingUrgent — get help

Heavy or spurting bleeding can become life-threatening within minutes.

Sickle cell diseaseUrgent — get help

Sickle cell disease makes red blood cells rigid and C-shaped, blocking small vessels and causing severe pain "crises" (often bones/back/chest), anemia and infections.

Sinusitis (sinus infection)See a doctor

Sinusitis is inflammation of the air-filled facial sinuses, usually after a cold: thick nasal discharge, facial pressure or pain (worse bending forward), blocked nose and reduced smell.

Skin rash / eczemaSelf-care

Dry itchy patches that come and go; often worsened by soaps and sweat.

Snake biteUrgent — get help

Keep the person calm and still; movement pumps venom faster.

Snoring & sleep apneaSee a doctor

Loud snoring with pauses/gasping in sleep and daytime sleepiness suggests obstructive sleep apnea — raises BP and heart risk.

Sore throat / tonsillitisSee a doctor

Most sore throats are viral and settle in 3–7 days.

Sprains & strainsSelf-care

Stretched or torn ligaments/muscles causing pain and swelling around a joint.

Stress managementSelf-care

Chronic stress harms sleep, BP and mood — managing it is a skill anyone can learn.

Stroke (brain attack)Urgent — get help

Use FAST: Face drooping, Arm weakness, Speech difficulty, Time to call emergency.

SunburnSelf-care

Overexposure to UV causing red, hot, painful skin, sometimes blisters.

T

TetanusUrgent — get help

Tetanus bacteria enter through wounds and cause painful muscle spasms starting with jaw stiffness.

Thalassemia basicsSee a doctor

Thalassemia is an inherited blood condition where the body makes less hemoglobin, causing anemia of varying degree.

Thyroid disordersSee a doctor

Low thyroid = weight gain, tiredness, cold intolerance.

Thyroid profile (TSH, T3, T4)See a doctor

TSH is the brain signal controlling the thyroid.

TinnitusSee a doctor

Ringing/buzzing with no external sound — very common; usually harmless but annoying.

Tobacco & quitting smokingSelf-care

Tobacco in any form (cigarette, bidi, gutkha, paan) causes cancer, heart and lung disease — every quit attempt counts.

Tuberculosis (TB)See a doctor

Cough over 2-3 weeks with night sweats, weight loss, evening fever.

Type 2 diabetes basicsSee a doctor

Diabetes means persistently high blood sugar; classic signs are excess thirst, frequent urination, fatigue.

TyphoidSee a doctor

Typhoid spreads through contaminated food/water; causes step-ladder rising fever over days with abdominal discomfort.

U

Urinary tract infection (UTI)See a doctor

Burning and urgency when passing urine, sometimes fever or back pain.

Urine routine examinationSee a doctor

A urine routine checks pus cells (infection markers), protein (kidney stress), sugar and ketones.

V

Vaginal discharge & thrushSee a doctor

Some clear/white odourless discharge is normal.

Varicose veinsSee a doctor

Twisted leg veins with aching/heaviness, worse standing; common in teachers, security, pregnancy.

Vertigo & dizzinessSee a doctor

A spinning sensation, often from inner-ear crystals (BPPV) — unsettling but rarely dangerous.

Vitamin B12 deficiencySee a doctor

Common in pure vegetarians and metformin users — fatigue, tingling hands/feet, poor memory, anaemia.

Vitamin D and B12 test rangesSee a doctor

Vitamin D below ~20 ng/mL is commonly treated as deficiency (bone pain, tiredness); B12 below ~200 pg/mL may cause tingling, fatigue and memory issues.

Vitamin D deficiencySelf-care

Very common in Indians despite sunshine — causes tiredness, aches and weak bones.

Vitiligo (white patches)See a doctor

Loss of skin colour in patches — a pigment issue, NOT contagious or "eating wrong foods".
